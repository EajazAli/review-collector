Google review rules

Built to play by Google's rules.

Google wants reviews that are genuine, unpaid and open to every customer. Review Finder is designed around that.

Every guest can post on GoogleGuests with a low rating see a "Post it to Google" option, with a second screen offering a direct line to the management as well.
Guests write their own wordsWe only copy what the guest typed. Nothing is written, edited or posted for them.
One review, one real visitThe QR code sits on the table, so reviews come from people who actually ate there.
No incentives for reviewsGoogle doesn't allow discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews. Don't offer them on your table cards.
No blocking negative reviewsGoogle prohibits stopping unhappy customers from reviewing ("review gating"). Review Finder never does.
No fake or staff reviewsOwners and staff shouldn't review their own business. Google removes these and can penalise the listing.

Summary for guidance only. See Google's own prohibited and restricted content policy for the full rules.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Review Finder post reviews to Google for my guests?

No. Google only accepts reviews written by the guest themselves. Review Finder copies the guest's comment and opens your Google review form, so they just tap the stars and paste. It takes them a few seconds.

What happens when a guest gives a low rating?

Their message, any photos and their contact details (if they choose to share them) go privately to your WhatsApp and your dashboard. You can reply to the guest on WhatsApp in one tap and mark the issue resolved.

Is this allowed under Google's review rules?

Yes, when it's used as designed. Google doesn't allow businesses to block or discourage negative reviews, so every guest, whatever their rating, sees a "Post it to Google" option. Lower ratings also get a direct line to the management, which is about fixing problems, not hiding them. Never offer rewards in exchange for reviews.

Do guests need to download an app or sign up?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and the review page opens in their browser.

What does "unlimited tables" mean?

One subscription covers the whole restaurant. Put the QR code on every table, menu, receipt or the entrance. There's no limit on scans or reviews.

Can I change my colours, logo or threshold later?

Yes. Log in to your dashboard and change them any time. Changes go live immediately, and your printed QR codes keep working.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp. We'll set up your review page, connect your Google review link and WhatsApp numbers, and send you your QR codes and dashboard login.
