Basics

Guests see the review page in Arabic or English automatically, based on their phone's language. They can also switch.

Client logins

Email addresses that can sign in and manage this business, one per line. They see only this business and its reviews.

To create a login: Supabase → Authentication → Users → Add user → Create new user, enter the same email and a password, tick Auto Confirm User. Then send the client the login page and their password.

Review threshold

Ratings at or above the threshold are sent to Google. Lower ratings go privately to the owner, so they get a second chance.

Theme

Google review link

In Google Business Profile, open Ask for reviews and copy the link. A Place ID (starts with ChIJ) also works.

Private feedback delivery

Low ratings, with photos, go to these WhatsApp numbers and to your inbox.

Social media

Shown on the thank-you screen. Use a handle (@name) or a full link.
